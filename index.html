Financial Econometrics & Statistics

1. Portfolio Risk & Asset-Class Optimization Analysis (ANOVA)

10-Year Historical Study (2015–2024) across 30 Asset Classes

  • 10-Year Historical Analysis: Evaluated historical return metrics for 30 diverse asset classes categorized under Low, Medium, and High risk portfolios across a decade of market cycles.
  • ANOVA & Variance Testing: Implemented Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) and descriptive statistical models to test for statistically significant performance divergence among risk groups.
  • Top Performing Asset Categories: Identified key category leaders:
    • Low Risk: Corporate Bonds (Average Return: 8.28%)
    • Medium Risk: ELSS Funds (Average Return: 14.96%)
    • High Risk: Technology Sector Funds (Average Return: 20.83%)
  • Statistical Significance Finding: Discovered a highly statistically significant variation within Low-Risk assets (F = 17.55, p < 0.001), whereas Medium and High-Risk groups demonstrated homogenous return variance (p = 0.323 and p = 0.898).
